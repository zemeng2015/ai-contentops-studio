# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free
experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give constructive technical feedback.
- Focus discussion on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Public or private sharing of someone else's private information.
- Repeated disruptive comments that derail project work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior is harmful
to the project community.
